Localization scope: navigation, layer labels, panel headings, status labels, empty/error states, alert controls, settings, My Intelligence, 24H Brief, Asset Intelligence, Timeline, Source Verification, Macro Calendar, Country Intelligence, Market Sessions, Time Machine, Impact Chain and share UI.

Source headlines remain in their original source language unless a trustworthy translated field exists in the source payload. ECRINTEL must not fabricate machine translations and attribute them to the source.

Numbers, market symbols, source names, URLs and timestamps remain data, not translated content. RTL languages must set document direction while preserving market symbols and numeric readability.
