## Full description
ECRINTEL brings global market intelligence into one focused mobile experience.

Monitor major markets alongside geopolitical, macroeconomic, energy and natural-event context. Explore a live world intelligence map, follow market radar data, review breaking developments, connect events to major assets, check key macro dates and trace intelligence back to its sources.

Core features:
• Global Intelligence Map for worldwide events and developments
• Market Radar for major global assets
• Breaking Intelligence for high-severity developments
• Asset Intelligence to connect assets with related public information
• Intelligence Timeline for chronological context
• Source Verification to surface source density and original sources
• Macro Calendar for major scheduled economic and central-bank events
• Market Impact view for observed relationships between events and markets

ECRINTEL is designed for information and research. It does not provide personalized investment advice, guaranteed returns or automated trading.
