ECRINTEL is a global market intelligence dashboard built to help you understand what is happening across markets, geopolitics, macroeconomics and energy — and how those developments relate to major assets.

Explore a live world map of current intelligence signals, follow market prices, review macro events and open source-backed event details from one mobile dashboard.

Key features:
• Global intelligence map
• Market radar for major global assets
• Breaking Intelligence alerts
• Asset Intelligence views
• Intelligence Timeline
• Source Verification indicators
• Macro Calendar
• Market Impact Engine
• Geopolitics, energy and macro filters
• Direct links to original public sources

ECRINTEL focuses on observed information and market context. It does not provide personalized investment advice, trade execution or guaranteed forecasts.
