Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

ECRINTEL is operated by Ecrin Labs. The app provides global market intelligence and public-information monitoring through the ECRINTEL web experience.

Information we collect

The current ECRINTEL Android app does not require an account and does not directly request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access or financial account credentials.

The app requires internet access to load ECRINTEL content and public data. Standard technical information such as IP address, device/browser information and request timestamps may be processed by hosting providers and websites you choose to open, as is normal for internet services.

Local storage

The app uses Android WebView storage and cache so the ECRINTEL interface can function correctly and load efficiently. This may include preferences and locally cached web resources on your device.

External sources and links

ECRINTEL may display or link to third-party news publishers, public-data providers, map services and other websites. When you open an external source, that third party's own privacy policy and practices apply.

Advertising

The current pre-production Android build does not include production advertising SDK credentials. If advertising such as Google AdMob is enabled in a production release, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before publication.

Financial information disclaimer

ECRINTEL is an information and research product. It does not provide personalized investment advice, guaranteed returns or automated trading.

Security

We use HTTPS for the ECRINTEL web experience and do not permit clear-text traffic in the Android application configuration.

Children

ECRINTEL is intended for a general market-information audience and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, its data sources or its third-party services change.
